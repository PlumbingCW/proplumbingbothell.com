Why inspect.

See the pipe before you decide anything

.

A sewer line runs underground for dozens of feet, and almost everything that goes wrong in it is out of sight. A camera inspection replaces guesswork with footage. You see roots, cracks, offsets and low spots, and you hear a plain explanation of what each one means.

It is the most useful single step before a repair, before a purchase and any time a drain keeps backing up.

What the camera can show

.
What you see.What it usually means.Next step.
Fine roots at a joint.A small opening that is feeding the roots.Clean the line and consider sealing the joint.
A long crack or break.Pipe damage that will let water and soil in.Spot repair, lining or replacement.
A sag filled with water.A low spot, often called a belly, where solids settle.Monitor, clean or correct depending on depth.
Offset joints.Two pipe sections have shifted apart.Repair at that joint.
A clean, intact pipe.The problem is probably upstream of the sewer.Check fixture drains and venting.

When an inspection is worth booking

.
  • You are buying a home and the seller has not had the line checked.
  • A drain has backed up more than once in a year.
  • You are planning a remodel, a new tree or work near the yard line.
  • You see wet or sunken ground over the sewer path.
  • A previous plumber cleared the line and you want to know what was causing the stoppage.

Local note.

Inspecting a Bothell house before you buy

.

Homes in Bothell range from mid-century houses to newer builds in places like Canyon Park and North Creek. A home inspector usually does not scope the sewer lateral. An inspection costs far less than a surprise during a remodel or after closing.

If the camera shows damage, you can ask the seller for repairs or a price change. If it shows a clean line, you know what you are buying. For repairs we offer sewer line repair and sewer line cleaning on the same visit if you want.

Where the footage shows work that falls short of the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as adopted in WAC 51-56, we say so plainly in the review.
