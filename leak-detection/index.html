Hidden water means damage is already happening

.

When a pipe leaks inside a wall, under a floor or in the yard, the water has to go somewhere. It soaks drywall, rots framing and feeds mold long before anything shows on the surface. The earlier we find it, the smaller the repair.

If you hear water running when nothing is on, see a stain spreading or smell damp, shut off the water at the main and call. If the meter keeps moving with the water off, the leak is real.

Signs there may be a hidden leak

.
  • The water bill jumps and your habits have not changed.
  • The meter dial or low-flow indicator moves with every fixture off.
  • Warm spots on a floor, which can point to a hot water line.
  • Musty smell, peeling paint, bubbling drywall or a stain that grows.
  • The sound of running or hissing water in a quiet house.
  • Low pressure at one or more fixtures.

Local note.

Reading your water bill in Bothell

.

The City of Bothell water page says it serves about 8,500 households and businesses, and four water districts serve other parts of Bothell. Check who serves your address before you call about a bill.

If you suspect a leak, the City of Bothell Utility Billing office handles leak and excess-use reports for city accounts. The City FAQ states that homeowners are responsible for repairs beyond the water meter, including the service line to the home. That is the part we can trace and fix.

Leak repairs follow the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as adopted in Washington under WAC 51-56 once the source is found.
