Why a leaking tank means replacement

.

Water heater tanks are steel vessels with a lining. When rust eats through the wall, the leak is permanent. A fitting or valve leak can be repaired, but a leak from the body of the tank means the heater is done.

We confirm the source before recommending anything. If it turns out to be a fitting, we will say so and repair it. See water heater repair for those cases.

Signs a tank is near the end of its life

.

A tank that is old, rusty at the bottom or making loud popping sounds is a risk. Rust-colored hot water, moisture at the base and a relief valve that drips are signs to watch. Water heaters often fail at night or on a holiday, which is why many people replace one before it breaks.

If your heater is more than ten years old and you have noticed any of these signs, plan the replacement instead of waiting for a leak. It lets you choose the unit and the time.

Local note.

Replacing a water heater in Bothell

.

The City of Bothell Permit Center says replacing a water heater requires a permit. A permit means the work is checked against the Washington plumbing code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code with state amendments. That matters for venting, relief piping and strapping.

Western Washington is seismically active, so strapping a water heater to the wall is a sensible requirement. If you are wondering about doing it yourself, read our water heater installation page. Permit and licensing rules vary, so ask the Permit Center and the state Department of Labor and Industries.

A replacement is installed to the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as adopted in WAC 51-56, including relief piping, strapping and venting.
