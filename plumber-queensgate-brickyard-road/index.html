The area.

Where Queensgate and Brickyard Road is

.

The City of Bothell lists Brickyard Road and Queensgate as the southeastern corner of the city. Interstate 405 bounds it on the west and SR 522 on the north. Brickyard Road NE is the main street through the area.

Blyth Park, the city's oldest park at 40 acres, was donated by the Lions Club in 1959. Brickyard Road Park is another named local park. These landmarks help us place a call quickly.

A mix of housing types

.

Real estate pages describe townhomes, mobile or manufactured homes and Craftsman-style houses in this area, on quiet streets with many cul-de-sacs. That is a description from listing sites and may not match your block.

The housing type changes what a plumber needs to know. Pipe access, shutoff locations and fixture style differ between a townhome, a manufactured home and a house. Tell us which you have when you call.

Same problem, different home

.

The first move can change with the housing type.

Townhome or condo

.
  • Check whether a shared drain or wall is involved.
  • Read association rules before booking.
  • Find the unit shutoff.

House or manufactured home

.
  • Find the main shutoff and the water heater first.
  • Look under the home if access exists, and note any damp areas.
  • Tell us the age and whether it has been updated.

Practical step.

Check who serves your address

.

Bothell has several water providers, and which one serves you depends on where you live. Look at your latest bill to see who sends it. That tells you who to call about the meter, the street side of the line and billing.

Everything on your side of the meter is yours to handle. That includes interior pipes, fixtures and the water heater. Use leak detection if the bill rises and nothing looks wrong.
