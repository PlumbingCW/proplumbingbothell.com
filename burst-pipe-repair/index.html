Stop the water in the next five minutes

.

You do not need a plumber to start limiting the damage. Do these in order while you wait for help.

  1. 1

    Shut off the main

    .

    Find the main water shutoff, usually where the line enters the house or at the meter box, and turn it fully off.

  2. 2

    Open a low faucet

    .

    Run a tap at the lowest level of the house to drain the pipes and ease the pressure.

  3. 3

    Cut power if water nears outlets

    .

    If water is near electrical outlets or appliances, switch off the breaker for that area. Do not stand in water to do it.

  4. 4

    Move belongings

    .

    Lift rugs, electronics and paper out of the wet area. Take photos for your records.

Then call, even if the water is off

.

Shutting off the water stops the flood, but the pipe is still broken and your water is off. Call and describe where the water was coming from and what the pipe looks like. A plumber can plan the right fittings and tell you what to expect.

If you cannot find the shutoff, tell us. We can usually talk you through where to look or what to do if the valve will not close.

What burst pipes tend to look like

.

A split along the pipe

.

Frozen water or age can split a copper or plastic line along its length. The leak is usually obvious.

A failed joint

.

Fittings, couplings and old solder joints give way under pressure and spray from the connection.

Pinholes in old copper

.

Small leaks appear at several points. This often signals a line near the end of its life.

Pressure damage

.

High water pressure puts stress on every joint. A sudden burst can follow years of pressure that was too high.

Repair the section or replace more?

.

The decision depends on what the rest of the pipe looks like.

Repair the failed part

.
  • The rest of the pipe is in good shape.
  • The failure was a single event, such as a freeze.
  • The pipe is accessible and not corroded.
  • You want the least disruption.

Replace a larger run

.
  • Several leaks or pinholes in the same line.
  • Corroded or brittle pipe.
  • Material known to fail, such as old galvanized.
  • A repair would be next to more weak points.

Local note.

Pipes, cold snaps and water pressure around Bothell

.

Western Washington winters are usually mild, but cold snaps do happen, and pipes in unheated crawl spaces, garages and exterior walls are the ones at risk. Insulate exposed lines before the cold arrives.

Bothell water comes from Seattle Public Utilities and the City of Bothell reports it as soft. Soft water is not a reason for pipe failure on its own. Age, pressure and freezing are. If your home has a pressure regulator near the main, ask us to check it when we repair the burst.

Replacement pipe and fittings are chosen to meet the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as adopted in Washington under WAC 51-56.
