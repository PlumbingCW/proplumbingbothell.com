The area.

Where Downtown Bothell sits

.

Downtown Bothell is on the north side of the Sammamish River, between the Westhill area and Interstate 405. It is the city's commercial center, with mixed-use buildings that put apartments above shops and restaurants.

Residential streets of older houses sit around that core. So a plumbing call from "downtown" can mean a sixth-floor apartment, a restaurant kitchen or a single-family house on a quiet street. The right first step is different for each one.

What has changed downtown, from published reports

.

The Urbanist reported in 2022 on a decade of rebuilding in the center of Bothell. These are the figures it gave.

  • The Merc on Main Street, rebuilt after a 2016 fire in the historic Mercantile Building, has 122 apartment homes.
  • A block of Main Street between 101st and 102nd Avenue NE opened to pedestrians in 2020.
  • Alexan, a six-story building at Bothell Way NE and SR 522, was described as having 368 apartments.
  • Samma Senior Apartments, near Bothell Landing Park, has 78 affordable units.

Project details may have changed since the article ran, so treat these as background, not as a current building directory.

Plumbing in an apartment, condo or mixed-use building

.

In a multi-unit building, the first question is who owns the pipe. A leak under your own sink is usually yours or your landlord's to deal with. A leak in a shared stack, a main shutoff or a common hallway usually belongs to the building.

Before you call anyone, find out whether your unit has its own shutoff valve and whether the building manager must authorize repairs. Water that crosses a wall or ceiling can affect neighbors, so tell the manager early and take photos.

History, then practical result.

Historic buildings and older pipe

.

Bothell grew around the Sammamish River, and its historic buildings are a real part of downtown. The Bothell Historical Museum sits in the Hannan House at the Park at Bothell Landing, and several pioneer homes were moved to that park.

This does not tell us what pipe is inside any particular building. If your building is older, the safe approach is to inspect it. A camera or a leak check shows more than a guess. See sewer inspection if a drain keeps backing up.
