First steps.

The main line is blocked: here is what to do tonight

.

When the main sewer line stops, the first sign is often a toilet that will not flush, a tub that fills when you run the washer or a basement floor drain that bubbles. Stop all water use in the house. Wastewater has nowhere to go and every fixture you run adds to the problem.

Look outside for a cleanout, a capped pipe near the house or in the yard. If you can see one, tell us where it is when you call. It speeds things up.

What usually blocks a main sewer line

.

The cause decides the method, so we start by asking what has been going on.

  • Tree and shrub roots that entered through joints and grew into a mat.
  • Grease that cooled and hardened in the pipe over time.
  • Wipes, paper towels and other items that do not break down.
  • A sag or offset in the pipe where solids settle.
  • Debris pushed into the line by a previous plumbing job.

Local note.

Sewer lines in Canyon Park, North Creek and Downtown Bothell

.

Neighborhoods built in different decades use different pipe. The older parts of Downtown Bothell and areas east of the river may have clay or cast iron, while newer subdivisions around Canyon Park and North Creek usually have plastic. Roots matter more in the older pipe, and grease matters in any line.

If your street is served by Northshore Utility District or by the City of Bothell, the public main is theirs and the pipe on your lot is yours. If water is backing up in the street, call the utility. If it is only in your house, call us. When roots keep returning, sewer line repair is the permanent answer.

We clean through approved access points and note any cleanout that does not meet the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as adopted in WAC 51-56.
