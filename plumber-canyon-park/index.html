The area.

What Canyon Park is

.

Canyon Park is on the northern edge of the city. The City of Bothell has planned it as a regional growth center, and the Puget Sound Regional Council counts it as one of 29 in the region. Most of the area is commercial and industrial.

Annexation in 1992 brought Canyon Park into the city and made Bothell a city in two counties. Canyon Park sits on the Snohomish County side. That annexation added about 11,400 people and nearly doubled the city's population.

Housing in and around Canyon Park

.

The city has worked to add homes near the jobs. A 2018 report described a proposed 118-townhouse project, Canyon Park 18 and 19, on about seven acres in the business park. Nearby residential areas include Canyon Creek and Crystal Springs, on either side of Canyon Park.

Real estate pages describe a mix of townhouses, houses and apartments, with a median year built around 1993. That figure comes from a listing site, so treat it as rough. It tells us the housing is newer than downtown, not what is inside any particular wall.

Townhome or suite: who handles what

.

Ownership of plumbing differs in attached homes and leased business space.

Attached home

.
  • The owner usually handles fixtures, supply lines and the water heater inside the unit.
  • Shared drains or walls may involve the HOA or neighbors.
  • Read your association rules before authorizing major work.

Business suite

.
  • The lease decides who repairs fixtures and who handles building systems.
  • The landlord or property manager often controls the main shutoff.
  • Tell the manager before you book a plumber.

Local context.

Employment center plus homes

.

The city's own plan has described Canyon Park as primarily an employment center, with strategies to add housing over time. That mix matters for plumbing because the same call center handles both a family's water heater and an office restroom.

If your property is a business, see commercial plumbing. If it is a home, the household services are the right place to start. Parks such as Centennial Park and North Creek Forest, a 64-acre preserve, are nearby landmarks.

Questions to answer before you call

.
  • Is it a home, an office or a retail suite?
  • Do you own the space or lease it?
  • Is there an HOA or a property manager to notify?
  • Where is the nearest shutoff valve?
