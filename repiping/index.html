Overview.

Why repiping is a planned project, not an emergency

.

Repiping replaces the water supply lines in your home. Done well, it ends a cycle of repairs on pipe that is wearing out everywhere at once. It is a planned job, so we focus on a clear scope, a clear price and as little disruption as possible.

If you have a leak right now, burst pipe repair is the right page. This one is for people who have had more than one leak, or who want to fix a known problem pipe before it fails.

Signs a repipe may be the right answer

.

Repeated pinhole leaks

.

Several small leaks in copper, especially in different rooms, suggest the whole line is thinning.

Brown or rusty water

.

Galvanized steel corrodes from the inside and can discolor the water.

Low pressure at many fixtures

.

Buildup inside old pipe narrows the opening.

Known problem pipe

.

Polybutylene, a gray plastic used in some homes decades ago, is prone to failure.

Pipe materials compared

.

We explain which fits your home. This is the short version.

Material.Strengths.Things to consider.
Copper.Long track record, rigid, heat tolerant.Higher material cost and more labor.
PEX.Flexible, fewer joints, quicker to install.Not for exposed sunlight, needs proper support.
Galvanized steel.Used in older homes.Corrodes inside, usually replaced rather than repaired.
Polybutylene.Used in some 1970s to 1990s homes.Known to fail, usually replaced.

Living through a repipe

.

A repipe is disruptive for a few days, but it is manageable with planning. Water is off for part of each day. We protect floors, open walls only where needed and leave the house usable at the end of each day when possible.

Plan for access to every room with a fixture, and move furniture and valuables away from walls we will open. Tell us about pets and children so we can plan the work safely.

Getting ready for a repipe

.
  • Walk through with us and note every fixture.
  • Clear closets and walls where we will work.
  • Choose a patching plan for drywall.
  • Arrange for water, a bathroom and meals during the work.
  • Confirm the permit and inspection dates.
  • Keep the invoice and permit copy for your records.
Local note.

Older Bothell homes and their pipes

.

Bothell grew quickly after 1950, and many neighborhoods have houses from the decades that followed. Homes from that period can have galvanized steel, copper or polybutylene supply lines depending on the year and the builder.

Bothell water is described by the City as soft, and the City reports that lead comes mainly from service lines and home plumbing. If your house still has older lines, ask us to identify the material. See water service line repair if the line from the meter is also in question.

Repiping is done to the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as adopted in Washington under WAC 51-56, which sets approved materials, support spacing and testing.
