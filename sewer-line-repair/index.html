If sewage is backing into the house, stop using water

.

A damaged sewer line does not clear with a cable. The water has nowhere to go, so stop running sinks, showers, the washer and the dishwasher until a plumber has looked at the line. Keep children and pets out of any area where wastewater has come up.

Call and tell us where the water is showing and whether a cleanout is visible outside. We can often get the line draining that night, then use a camera to see what failed.

Signs the pipe itself is failing

.

A blocked line and a broken line can look the same at first. These details separate them.

Backups that return after cleaning

.

If a drain is cleared and slows down again within weeks, the pipe may have a belly or an open joint that keeps catching debris.

Sewage smell in the yard

.

A damp, foul-smelling patch of lawn or a sunken spot over the line can mean a leak in the pipe.

Multiple fixtures gurgling

.

Air is being pushed back through the traps because water cannot clear the main.

Cracks or roots on camera

.

The camera is the only way to see inside the buried pipe and decide between repair options.

Repair options compared

.

The right option depends on the type of damage, the depth and what is on top of the pipe.

Option.Fits.Trade-off.
Spot repair.One cracked or offset section.Digging is limited, but it only fixes the damaged part.
Pipe lining.Long runs with small cracks or open joints.Less digging, but the pipe needs to be cleaned and round enough to line.
Full replacement.Collapsed, badly rooted or very old pipe.More disruption, but a new line that should not fail for decades.

Because a line must be clear before a camera or liner can pass through, many repairs begin with sewer line cleaning.

Local note.

Who owns what in Bothell

.

Sewer service in Bothell comes from the City of Bothell or Northshore Utility District, depending on the address. Both connect to the King County regional system. The pipe from your house to the connection is generally your responsibility, so check the service map for your street if you are unsure.

Many Bothell homes date from the decades after 1950, and older lateral materials such as clay or Orangeburg can fail near joints. Ask the Bothell Permit Center whether your repair needs a permit before work starts. We can tell you what we have to file.

Sewer repairs follow the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) with Washington amendments in WAC 51-56, which covers pipe material, slope and cleanouts.
