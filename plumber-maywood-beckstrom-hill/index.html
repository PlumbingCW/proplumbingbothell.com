The area.

Where Maywood and Beckstrom Hill is

.

The City of Bothell groups this area as Maywood and Beckstrom Hill. It lies north of downtown and east of the Bothell-Everett Highway, also known as SR 527. Boundaries are not sharply marked, so we describe it by those anchors.

The name Beckstrom has a documented history here. The Beckstrom Cabin, built in 1883, was moved to the Park at Bothell Landing in 1979, where visitors can see it today. That history does not tell us what pipe is in any house on the hill.

A mostly single-family area

.

City-Data's statistics for Maywood and Beckstrom Hill show that about 64 percent of housing units were detached houses in its 2021 data. Those figures are estimates from a neighborhood statistics site, so use them as a rough guide only.

In a house you own, you are responsible for the plumbing from the water meter inward. That includes the water heater, fixtures, supply lines and the drain line. It makes a planned maintenance habit more valuable than it would be in a rental or a condo.

A yearly plumbing check for a single-family house

.

These are general tasks for any house. They are not a claim about local conditions.

  • Test the main shutoff valve and make sure it turns.
  • Look at the water heater base, the relief valve and the pipes for drips.
  • Check under sinks and behind toilets for slow leaks.
  • Run every drain and note any that are slow.
  • Note your water pressure, and ask about a gauge if it seems high or low.

Permits and who to call

.

Replacing a water heater in Bothell requires a permit, and other work may too. Ask the City of Bothell Permit Center what your job needs before work starts. We will tell you what we know about your project.

If water is running and you cannot find the cause, leak detection can trace it without opening walls first. For an aging tank, see water heater installation to understand what a swap involves.
